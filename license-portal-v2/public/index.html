Self-service renewal

For hospital IT

Pay once a month.
Renew without waiting on anyone.

You pay for the next month from when the current license ends — the same way Starlink or Netflix renews. HMS then picks up the paid month itself.

1

Pay here

Card or mobile money via Paystack. Invoice is created immediately; receipt after payment succeeds.

2

HMS applies it

When the paid month starts, HMS pulls the license automatically. Or open License in HMS and click Renew from portal. Download JSON remains a backup.

3

Give finance the PDFs

Invoice and receipt are for management. They are not needed to activate HMS.

If you paid early, HMS keeps the current month until the new one starts. Do not replace a live file with a future month.
