## Frequently Asked Questions

### **Q: Can I switch plans later?**
A: Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

### **Q: What happens to my data if I cancel?**
A: You can export all your data before cancellation. We retain backups for 30 days after cancellation.

### **Q: Is there a setup fee?**
A: No setup fee for Standard and Professional plans. Enterprise plan includes free setup and migration.

### **Q: Do you offer on-premise installation?**
A: Currently, we only offer cloud-based SaaS. Contact us for on-premise options (custom pricing).

### **Q: What about data security?**
A: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We comply with healthcare data protection standards.

### **Q: Can I customize the system?**
A: Enterprise plan includes custom development options. Contact us for specific requirements.

### **Q: Are there any hidden fees?**
A: No hidden fees. The monthly subscription includes all features listed for your plan, hosting, backups, and standard support.

### **Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept bank transfers, mobile money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo), and credit/debit cards.

### **Q: Is there a minimum contract period?**
A: Minimum subscription period is 3 months. After that, you can cancel with 30 days written notice.

### **Q: Do prices include taxes?**
A: All prices exclude applicable taxes (VAT, etc.). Taxes will be added as per Ghanaian tax regulations.
